# Roasted Vegetable Quinoa Bowl

Synthetic fixture recipe, written for Chef's test suite.

## Ingredients

- 150 g quinoa
- 2 carrot, sliced
- 1 zucchini, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil

## Steps

1. Roast the carrot and zucchini in the oil at 180 C for 50 minutes.
2. Cook the quinoa in salted water until tender.
3. Serve the vegetables over the quinoa.
