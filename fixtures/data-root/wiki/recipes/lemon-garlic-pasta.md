# Lemon Garlic Pasta

Synthetic fixture recipe, written for Chef's test suite.

## Ingredients

- 200 g spaghetti
- 3 clove garlic
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon
- 30 g parmesan

## Steps

1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until just tender.
2. Warm the oil and sliced garlic over low heat until fragrant.
3. Toss the drained pasta with the garlic oil, lemon zest and juice, and parmesan.
