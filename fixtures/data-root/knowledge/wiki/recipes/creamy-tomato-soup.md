# Creamy Tomato Soup

Synthetic fixture recipe, written for Chef's test suite.

## Ingredients

- 800 g crushed tomatoes
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 carrot, diced
- 200 ml cream
- 500 ml vegetable stock

## Steps

1. Soften the onion and carrot in a pot.
2. Add the tomatoes and stock and simmer gently for 50 minutes.
3. Blend smooth and stir in the cream.
