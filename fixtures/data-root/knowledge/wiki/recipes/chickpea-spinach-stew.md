# Chickpea Spinach Stew

Synthetic fixture recipe, written for Chef's test suite.

## Ingredients

- 2 can chickpeas, drained
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 clove garlic
- 400 g crushed tomatoes
- 150 g spinach
- 1 tsp ground cumin

## Steps

1. Soften the onion and garlic in a pot.
2. Add the cumin, tomatoes and chickpeas and simmer for 25 minutes.
3. Stir in the spinach until it wilts.
